Billing and Collections Guide

Payment status values
A bill is Paid when payment is received, Pending when it is issued but not yet due or not yet settled, and Overdue when the due date has passed without payment.

Unpaid rate
The unpaid rate is the share of bills that are Pending or Overdue. A high unpaid rate reduces cash flow, so it is a key indicator for finance and collections teams.

Pending versus overdue
Pending bills need a reminder, while Overdue bills need follow-up. Treating them separately helps the team focus on the accounts that carry the most risk.

Typical collection actions
Common practice is to send reminders by SMS or email, then call the customer, then offer a payment plan, and only after notice restrict services. Postpaid customers with large overdue amounts are usually contacted first.

Missing bill amounts
A bill with no amount cannot be collected or analysed. Missing amounts should be investigated with the billing system owner, because they may point to a failed invoice run rather than a customer issue.
