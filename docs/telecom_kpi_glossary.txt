Telecom KPI Glossary

ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)
ARPU is total revenue divided by the number of users in a period, usually one month. Comparing ARPU between postpaid and prepaid plans shows which segment earns more per subscriber.

ARPU and missing bill amounts
If some bill amounts are missing, ARPU is calculated only on the bills that have a value. A report should state how many bills were excluded, because missing amounts can make ARPU look higher or lower than the true figure.

Churn rate
Churn is the share of subscribers who leave in a period. Prepaid churn is harder to measure than postpaid churn, because prepaid users can stop recharging without formally cancelling.

Data usage per user
This is the average number of GB a subscriber uses in a month. The mean is pulled up by a few very heavy users, so the median is often a better picture of a typical subscriber. Reports should show both.

Minutes of Use (MoU)
MoU is the average number of call minutes per subscriber per month. A falling MoU together with rising data usage is a common sign that customers are moving from calls to data and messaging apps.
