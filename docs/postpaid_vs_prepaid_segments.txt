Postpaid and Prepaid Segments

Postpaid customers
Postpaid customers receive a bill after use. They typically have higher data usage, more call minutes, and a higher ARPU than prepaid customers, and they carry credit risk if a bill is not paid.

Prepaid customers
Prepaid customers pay before use through recharges. They usually have lower ARPU and lower usage, they carry no credit risk, and they churn more easily because they can switch operators without notice.

Comparing segments
When comparing the two segments, look at ARPU, average data usage, and unpaid rate together. A segment with high ARPU but a high unpaid rate may earn less than it appears.

Using the analysis for action
Postpaid customers with a high unpaid rate are candidates for collections follow-up. Prepaid customers with low and falling usage are candidates for retention offers such as data boosters or bundled recharges.
